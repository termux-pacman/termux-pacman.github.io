If you would like to help support the development of the project, you may consider making a donation. The donation will help pay for services/hosts, and will pay developers for their work at [@termux-pacman](https://github.com/termux-pacman).

<br>
You can make a donation through [OpenCollective](https://opencollective.com) or through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors):
 - ### OpenCollective
   Become our OpenCollective contributor by making a monthly, yearly or one-time donation using PayPal, debit/credit card or US bank transfer - [opencollective.com/termux-pacman](https://opencollective.com/termux-pacman)
 - ### GitHub Sponsors
   Become our GitHub sponsor by making a monthly or one-time donation using PayPal or debit/credit card - [github.com/sponsors/termux-pacman](https://github.com/sponsors/termux-pacman)

## Termux
[@termux](https://github.com/termux) isn't just an environment where you can use pacman, it's also one of the main partners of [@termux-pacman](https://github.com/termux-pacman). Termux supplies and supports the builder and scripts that allow to build packages in the pacman format. Support them! Learn more on their website - [termux.dev/donate](https://termux.dev/donate)
